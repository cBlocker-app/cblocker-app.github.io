How it works

  1. You choose what to block: two categories, social media and adult sites, plus any sites you add by hand.
  2. You set when you can visit: one schedule per category, with the days and hours you choose.
  3. You turn on commitment mode for 7, 14 or 30 days. During that time your settings can only be made stricter.
  4. If you want in early, you wait at least 3 minutes while seeing a text you wrote yourself, retype your personal phrase and decide: keep blocked or unlock.
  5. Unlocking opens a 30-minute window. Only one unlock is allowed per day.

Privacy

The sites you visit are processed in your browser and never leave it. Your personal text and phrases are stored only on your device. CBlocker has no server of its own: it only checks your license key with the license service of our payment provider, which records the key and the devices where you activate it, never your browsing history.

What CBlocker does not do

CBlocker is a commitment barrier, not a wall. You can disable or uninstall the extension from your browser, and neither the wait nor the phrase prevents that. To work in incognito windows you must allow it yourself. It only protects the browser where it is installed, not your phone. It is not medical or psychological treatment: if your habit affects you, consider talking to a professional.
