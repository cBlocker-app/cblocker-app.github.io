Cómo funciona

  1. Eliges qué bloquear: páginas concretas o categorías, como redes sociales o páginas para adultos.
  2. Defines cuándo puedes entrar: días y horarios, con las ventanas que quieras.

How it works

  1. You choose what to block: specific pages or categories, such as social media or adult sites.
  2. You set when you can visit: days and times, with as many windows as you like.
  3. You turn on commitment mode for 7, 14 or 30 days. During that time your settings can only be made stricter.
  4. If you want in early, you wait at least 3 minutes while seeing the image or text you chose, retype your phrase and decide: keep blocked or unlock.
  5. Unlocking opens a 30-minute window. Only one unlock is allowed per day.

You decide what unlocking costs

When you set up, you choose no cost (0 USD) or a payment of 10, 15 or 20 USD for each unlock. If you turn it on, you cannot lower it while commitment mode lasts. Breaking your own rule costs, and that cost is real.

Planned pricing

  • Activation: 24.90 USD, one-time payment.
  • Early unlock: 0, 10, 15 or 20 USD, as you choose.
  • Maximum weekly spend: seven times your chosen cost, since there is one unlock per day.
  • 15-day refund on the activation.

Prices in US dollars. They may change before launch.

Privacy

The sites you visit are processed in your browser and never leave it. Your personal image, text and phrases are stored only on your device. Our server keeps only your license key, your payment status and the time of paid unlocks, never your browsing history.

What CBlocker does not do

CBlocker is a commitment barrier, not a wall. You can disable or uninstall the extension from your browser, and neither the cost nor the wait prevents that. To work in incognito windows you must allow it yourself. It only protects the browser where it is installed, not your phone. It is not medical or psychological treatment: if your habit affects you, consider talking to a professional.
